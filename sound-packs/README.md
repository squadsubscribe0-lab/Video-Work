# Sound Packs

Free sound effects for video editing. Everything here is **CC0 (public domain)** — free for personal and commercial videos, no credit required (crediting Kenney / kenney.nl is appreciated).

All files are 16-bit WAV, 44.1 kHz — they import directly into CapCut, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects and Shotcut.

| Pack | Files | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| `kenney-interface-sounds/` | 100 | Pop-ups, text/title reveals, menu transitions, glitch cuts, error/success moments, ticks for counters |
| `kenney-ui-audio/` | 51 | Clicks, switches and hover sounds for UI showcases and tutorial/screen-recording videos |

## Quick picks for editing

- **Title or text pop-in:** `kenney-interface-sounds/maximize/`, `open/`, `pluck/`
- **Text or element pop-out:** `kenney-interface-sounds/minimize/`, `close/`
- **Glitch transition:** `kenney-interface-sounds/glitch/`, `scratch/`
- **Success / win / reward:** `kenney-interface-sounds/confirmation/`, `bong/`, `glass/`
- **Fail / wrong answer:** `kenney-interface-sounds/error/`
- **Counter or timer ticking:** `kenney-interface-sounds/tick/`
- **Scrolling / list movement:** `kenney-interface-sounds/scroll/`
- **Button clicks in tutorials:** `kenney-ui-audio/click/`, `mouseclick/`, `kenney-interface-sounds/click/`
- **Toggles and switches:** `kenney-ui-audio/switch/`, `kenney-interface-sounds/toggle/`
